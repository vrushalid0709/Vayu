About VAYU

VAYU is a localized air quality forecasting and advisory platform built using government-validated data from monitoring stations near MIT–Kothrud.

The system analyzes daily AQI and pollutant trends and uses a lightweight machine learning model to predict the next day’s air quality risk. This short-term focus ensures reliable and practical forecasts.

When unsafe conditions are expected, VAYU generates early warnings and translates predictions into clear health advisories using an AI-assisted layer.

The name VAYU, derived from the Sanskrit word for air, reflects the goal of making air quality risks visible and understandable.
